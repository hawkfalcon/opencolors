About opencolors
The palette studio that’s free forever.

      
    

    

      
No accounts. Your palettes live in your browser, not our database (we don’t have one).

      
No paywall. Image palettes, vision simulator, 9-color palettes — all free, forever.

      
Actually open. Icons are Feather Icons (open-source, MIT-licensed). Color names blend an original dictionary with the public-domain xkcd color survey (CC0); starter presets are top community palettes via nice-color-palettes (MIT). No borrowed assets.

      
Private by design. No tracking, no uploads. It even works offline.

      
Keyboard-first. Space generate · L/1–9 lock · C copy · ←→ reorder · Ctrl Z undo/Shift Z redo · ? all shortcuts
